About OmniFabric

Built on a heritage of sovereign cloud and AI-native data

OmniFabric unites two proven foundations — sovereign cloud infrastructure and an AI-native data platform — into one fabric for data and AI.

Our mission

Make trusted AI the default — on infrastructure you control

Every organization should be able to build production AI on data it owns, with decisions it can audit. OmniFabric brings sovereign cloud and an AI-native data platform together, so teams ship trusted agents without surrendering control of their data.

Cloud heritage

CloudSigma

Sovereign, flexible cloud infrastructure

Founded in 2009 in Zürich, Switzerland by Robert Jenkin and Patrick Baillie, CloudSigma pioneered a pure IaaS where customers define their own infrastructure with no artificial restrictions.

Operating from Zurich, Las Vegas and Washington and serving more than 75,000 customers, CloudSigma delivers secure, sustainable and sovereign Cloud-as-a-Service — and now Intelligence-as-a-Service.

Data & AI heritage

MatrixOrigin

An AI-native data intelligence platform

Founded in 2021 with teams in Shanghai, Shenzhen, Beijing and the Bay Area, MatrixOrigin builds MatrixOne — an AI-native, MySQL-compatible HTAP database with Git-for-Data version control and built-in vector and full-text search.

Its hyper-converged engine unifies transactional, analytical, vector and full-text workloads in one system — no ETL — paired with MatrixOne Intelligence, a one-stop Data + AI platform.
