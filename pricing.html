FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Use hourly pay-as-you-go for spiky, short-lived or experimental workloads — you're billed per second with no commitment. Reserve monthly nodes for steady production and save around 20%. You can mix both in the same project and switch a node between the two at any time.

Every model call is metered by input and output tokens and priced per million. Use OmniFabric's routed models, or bring your own provider keys — in which case tokens pass through at the provider's cost with no markup, while still wrapped in the same lineage and governance.
