Four core modules

The full pipeline, end to end

From ingestion to retrieval agents — building the next-generation multimodal data intelligence platform.

Unified ingestion, storage & indexing

  • Data source integration — multi-source connectors for structured, semi-structured and unstructured data.
  • Automated ingestion — historical and incremental loads with completeness and real-time freshness.

Multimodal parsing & extraction

  • Complex document parsing — extract structured information with multiple parsing, chunking and vectorization strategies.
  • Multimedia parsing — images, audio and video with swappable model support.

Flexible data processing workflows

  • Built-in operators — pre-built parsing, cleansing and augmentation nodes, plus custom node support.
  • Visual orchestration — DAG-based workflow configuration for rapid pipeline setup.

Data retrieval & analytics agents

  • Data Q&A agent — natural-language queries across structured and unstructured data, with aggregation and drill-down.
  • Full-chain traceability — answers include complete lineage of sources, reasoning and execution steps.
