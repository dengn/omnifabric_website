How it works

From evaluation to production in three steps

1

Connect your data

Point OmniFabric at your databases, files, and streams. It ingests, parses, and embeds everything into one governed fabric — no separate vector store required.

2

Compose your agents

Wire any model into agents with built-in memory and skills. Every call is snapshotted, so behavior is explainable from day one.

3

Ship & self-improve

Run safe A/B tests on live data, gate changes behind regressions, and let agents refine themselves — with rollback always one click away.
